Helpful mail, with one clear next step.

A calm transactional system for Hey Ralli. Every email has one job: state what changed, give the recipient one useful action, then get out of the way.

Recognizable at a glanceForest header, white canvas, quiet green footer.
Short by defaultHeadline, one or two sentences, one primary action.
Ready for real inboxesBuilt later as table-based Resend templates with plain-text companions.
